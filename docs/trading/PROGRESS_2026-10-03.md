## Safety boundary

The production live flag remains disabled. The production coordinator is therefore not executable against Binance while the flag is false. The coordinator also stops on the TradingControl emergency stop, an uncleared circuit breaker, an unreviewed Shariah policy, or a failed Binance production preflight.

The coordinator deliberately does **not** mark a real-money trade as settled in the GameVortex wallet. Exchange execution, position closure, realized P/L verification, and internal wallet settlement are separate accounting events and must not be fabricated from an entry fill alone.

## Important external constraint

The application-level minimum remains **$1**, but Binance can impose symbol-specific minimum-notional and quantity/price filters. Those checks are now part of the live execution path. Therefore a requested $1 trade is still the application's minimum, but a specific Binance symbol can legitimately reject it if its current exchange rules require a larger notional.
